    WELCOME TO THE ROOM

    Make your best five.

    Combine your two private cards with the five community cards to make the strongest five-card hand.

    Check to pass when no bet is owed. Call to match a bet, raise to increase it, or fold to leave the hand. All in commits all your remaining chips when the betting rules allow it.

    Community cards arrive in three stages: flop (3), turn (1), river (1). The last player standing or best hand at showdown wins the pot.

    Strongest to weakest
    Straight flush → Four of a kind → Full house → Flush → Straight → Three of a kind → Two pair → Pair → High card

    Each player begins with 1,000 chips. Blinds are 10 / 20. The dealer rotates every hand. In solo play, choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or GTO above the table. Changes take effect next hand. In online rooms, the host deals each hand and can add or remove Medium AI players before the first deal. AI seats are labeled AI. Each turn has a 45-second limit: a timeout checks when possible, otherwise folds. New players join before the first hand; returning players can reconnect to their seat. In Direct connection mode, refreshing or closing the host tab ends the room. Server rooms let you return to your seat from the same browser tab.
